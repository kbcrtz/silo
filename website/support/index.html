Common questions

How do I cancel my membership?

Memberships are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Silo. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

I got a new phone. How do I get my Silo back?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before. Your colors, measurements and saved pieces come back with it. If the app asks you to subscribe again, tap Restore purchases on that screen.

My color result looks wrong.

Retake the selfie facing a window in daylight, without filters or tinted glasses. You can redo it any time from Profile, under Redo onboarding.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. Everything Silo stores about you is deleted. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.
