Your data

Privacy policy.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Silo is an iPhone app that finds your colors from a selfie and shows you clothes in them. This policy covers what the app collects, why, who helps us run it, and how to delete your data. Questions go to getsiloapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We use your data to run Silo, and for nothing else.
  • We don't sell it, show ads, or track you across other apps.
  • Until you create an account, everything except your selfie analysis stays on your phone.
  • Deleting your account in the app deletes everything we store about you.

What we collect

Your selfie

To find your colors, the app sends your selfie to our server, which strips its metadata and passes a smaller copy to Google's Gemini AI with storage turned off. We don't keep the original. Once you create an account, a small copy (about 480 pixels wide) is saved as the photo on your Silo passport. You can change it in Profile.

Your profile

The name you put on your passport, your age and gender, whether you shop women's or men's, your answers to the color questions and your color result, and the fit you prefer.

Your measurements

Height, weight and the body measurements you enter, with their history, so we can recommend sizes and draw a mannequin with your proportions.

Your style and what you save

The looks you pick, your saved pieces, lists and outfits, and a running score of the looks and garments you tend to save, open or shop, which we use to order your feed.

Activity in the app

When you open, save or tap Shop on a piece, we record which piece, your color season, and the piece's garment type and color. We use it to mark what's popular with people in your season. These records are deleted after 30 days.

Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an account ID and, if the provider shares it, your email address.

Your membership

Apple handles payment. RevenueCat tells us whether your membership is active. We never see your card or payment details.

Who helps us run Silo

  • Supabase stores your account and the data above.
  • Railway hosts our server.
  • Google Gemini analyzes selfies for color, reads product listings, and draws try-on images. For try-ons it gets the product photo, an outline of your proportions and a word for your build, never your selfie or your measurements.
  • RevenueCat keeps track of memberships.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in.
  • Shopify supplies the products in your feed. It receives search words such as "women's sage knit sweater", never anything about you.

Each of them handles your data only to provide their service to us. When you tap Shop, the store's own website opens and its own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. App activity records are deleted after 30 days. Try-on images are deleted after 7 days, and right away if you delete your account. The app also keeps its own copy of your data on your phone, which is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. This deletes your account and everything we store about you, and if you signed in with Apple, it revokes Silo's access to your Apple ID. You can also email getsiloapp@gmail.com and we'll delete it for you. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your membership; cancel that in your iPhone's Settings, under Subscriptions.

Children

Silo isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has used Silo, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app first.
